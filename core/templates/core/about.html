About Heritage Atlas India

India is not just a country. It is a collection of stories.

Heritage Atlas India is a digital journey through the places, traditions, festivals, crafts, food and history that shape the cultural identity of India.

Our idea

Making heritage easier to explore.

India has an extraordinary cultural heritage spread across its states and communities. Every region has its own stories, celebrations, artistic traditions, flavours and historic places.

Heritage Atlas India brings these different elements together in one digital space, allowing visitors to explore India state by state.

The goal is not simply to provide information. It is to create a visual and interactive experience that encourages people to discover, understand and appreciate India's diverse heritage.

Heritage Atlas India aims to make cultural exploration engaging, accessible and meaningful through a modern digital experience.
